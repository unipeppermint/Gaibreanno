Time Cards

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Time Cards is an offline card strategy game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of Time Cards.

1. Information Collection

Time Cards does not collect or transmit personal information to us during gameplay. No account, name, email address, or other personal details are required to play.

The app does not include advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. Local Game Data

The app stores the following information on your device:

  • Your card collection and selected deck.
  • Level completion, difficulty selections, and star ratings.
  • Your current battle and battle history.
  • Preferences, including haptic feedback and reduced motion.

This information is used to save your progress, resume battles, and apply your preferences. We do not receive or have remote access to your saved game data.

3. Data Sharing and Backups

We do not sell, rent, or share your gameplay data or use it for advertising.

Time Cards does not provide its own cloud storage service. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Apple or your computer. These backups are governed by the services you use.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data is stored locally until the app and its data are deleted or the data is otherwise removed.

To remove local game data, choose Delete App in iOS Settings. Choosing Offload App retains the app’s documents and data.

Deleting the app may not remove data from existing device backups. You can manage backups through the relevant Apple or computer backup settings. Restoring a backup may restore your saved game data.

5. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond to your request and resolve support issues.

Support messages are processed through our email service provider. We retain them only as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the email address below.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in support messages.

6. Children’s Privacy

Time Cards does not collect personal information from players through the app, including children, and does not contain advertising or tracking.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through a support message, please contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or information-handling practices change. Updates will be reflected in the effective date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

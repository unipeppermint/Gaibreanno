Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished high-fidelity product prototype image for an ORIGINAL native iOS portrait card puzzle game called “叠时档案”. This is an interface design presentation, not a poster. One large landscape board with EXACTLY THREE complete, equally sized, upright iPhone UI screens aligned side by side, ordered left to right: archive library, card puzzle worktable, completed restoration. Each screen has realistic 390:844 proportions, clean thin rounded device edge, native iOS status bar at 9:41, safe areas and home indicator. Flat front view, no perspective. Render high resolution, approximately 2400 x 1600 or larger, all three screens fully visible. Quiet warm neutral background outside the screens, generous gutters, small captions beneath each screen: “01 档案柜”, “02 修复桌”, “03 修复完成”.

Visual design: sophisticated editorial archive aesthetic with ivory paper, subtle very fine paper grain, dark charcoal legible text, muted cyan-blue timeline accents, restrained cinnabar red archive stamps. Rich yet restrained hand-painted gouache story illustrations, coherent across all three screens. Rounded rectangular collectible story cards with fine borders and subtle tactile shadows. Primary controls dark desaturated teal. Native-feeling touch sizes and spacious margins. Large elegant Chinese serif titles paired with crisp readable Chinese sans-serif UI labels. Light mode. All Simplified Chinese text must be clear, accurate, and typeset, with no gibberish. Avoid dense paragraphs, fake tiny text, purple gradients, glossy casino visuals, medieval fantasy, excessive ornaments and fantasy card-game stats.
